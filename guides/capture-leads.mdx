Lead capture is the foundation of every successful conversion strategy. By placing the right form or pop-up in front of visitors at the right moment, you collect the contact details you need to start a conversation and move prospects through your funnel. This guide walks you through every step — from choosing a capture method to publishing your first form and embedding it on your site.

## Choose a Capture Method

Google offers three ways to capture leads. Choose the method that best fits your page layout and visitor behavior.

**Inline Form** — Embed a form directly within a page's content. Inline forms work well on high-intent pages such as pricing, contact, or landing pages, where visitors are already looking for a way to reach you.

**Pop-Up** — Display a form triggered by a specific visitor behavior. Pop-ups are ideal for capturing leads who are about to leave or who have demonstrated engagement by scrolling or spending time on your page. Available triggers include:

- **Exit Intent** — fires when the visitor's cursor moves toward the browser's close button or address bar
- **Time on Page** — fires after the visitor has been on the page for a set number of seconds (e.g., 30 seconds)
- **Scroll Depth** — fires when the visitor scrolls past a defined percentage of the page (e.g., 50%)

**Landing Page** — A standalone, Google-hosted page built entirely around a single conversion goal. No website required — just share the URL.

## Create Your First Form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Form Builder">
    Go to **Capture → Forms → New Form** in your Google dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Starting Point">
    Select a pre-built template matched to your goal (Newsletter Sign-Up, Book a Demo, Download a Resource) or click **Start from Blank** to build from scratch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and Arrange Fields">
    Click **Add Field** to insert any of the following field types:

    - **Name** — single-line text
    - **Email** — validated email input
    - **Phone** — formatted phone number input
    - **Custom Field** — text, dropdown, checkbox, or date

    Drag fields up or down to reorder them. Mark any field as **Required** by toggling the switch next to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Trigger (Pop-Ups Only)">
    If you chose the Pop-Up method, open the **Trigger** tab and select one of the following:

    - **Exit Intent** — activates when the cursor exits the viewport
    - **Time on Page** — enter a delay in seconds (e.g., `30`)
    - **Scroll Depth** — enter a scroll percentage (e.g., `50`)

    You can combine triggers using **OR** logic so the pop-up fires on whichever condition is met first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize Copy and Appearance">
    Update the **Headline**, **Subheadline**, **CTA Button Text**, and **Success Message** under the **Content** tab. Switch to the **Design** tab to match your brand colors and font.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and Deploy">
    Click **Publish** in the top-right corner. Google will present two options:

    - **Embed Code** — copy the snippet and paste it into your site (see below)
    - **Enable Pop-Up** — toggle the pop-up on for the URLs you specify in the **Display Rules** panel
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Embed an Inline Form on Your Site

After publishing, copy your form's embed code from the **Embed** tab and add it to any page on your site. Replace `FORM_ID` with the ID shown in your dashboard.

```html
<div id="google-form" data-form-id="FORM_ID"></div>
<script src="https://cdn.google.com/forms.js"></script>
```

The script loads asynchronously and will not affect your page's load time. The form renders responsively and adapts to any container width.

## Setting Up Exit Intent

Exit intent detection tracks the velocity and direction of the visitor's mouse. When the cursor accelerates toward the top edge of the browser window — the typical motion before closing a tab or typing a new URL — Google fires the pop-up trigger.

**To enable exit intent:**

1. Open your pop-up form and go to the **Trigger** tab.
2. Select **Exit Intent** from the trigger list.
3. Set a **Session Cooldown** (e.g., 24 hours) so the same visitor does not see the pop-up on every page view.
4. Optionally, restrict the trigger to specific pages using **Display Rules → URL contains**.

**Best practices for exit intent pop-ups:**

- Lead with value, not alarm — offer a discount, free resource, or clear benefit rather than a generic "Wait, don't go!" headline.
- Keep the form to one or two fields. The visitor is already leaving; friction will cost you the conversion.
- Test your headline copy using A/B testing in **Campaigns → A/B Tests**.

<Tip>
  Forms with two or three fields consistently outperform longer forms. Collect only the data you need to start the conversation — you can capture additional details later through your follow-up workflow.
</Tip>
