Campaigns let you send the right message to the right audience at exactly the right moment in their journey. Whether you are nurturing cold leads, re-engaging dormant contacts, or guiding prospects toward a purchase, Google's campaign builder gives you the sequencing, personalization, and testing tools to do it effectively. By the end of this guide, you will have a fully configured campaign ready to go live.

## Create a Campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Campaign Builder">
    Go to **Campaigns → New Campaign** in your Google dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Campaign Type">
    Select the channel that best fits your goal:

    - **Email** — single or multi-step email sequences
    - **SMS** — short text message campaigns for time-sensitive offers or reminders
    - **Drip** — a mixed-channel sequence that combines email and SMS steps with delays between them
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name Your Campaign and Set a Goal">
    Give the campaign a descriptive internal name (e.g., "Q3 Demo Request Nurture"). Then choose a **Goal** from the dropdown — this is the conversion event that marks a contact as converted and removes them from the active sequence:

    - Book a Demo
    - Complete Purchase
    - Download Resource
    - Custom Goal (any event you define in **Settings → Goals**)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Your Audience">
    Click **Set Audience** and filter your contact list using one or more of the following criteria:

    - **Tag** — e.g., `new-lead`, `trial-user`, `enterprise`
    - **Status** — Active, Unsubscribed, Converted
    - **Lead Source** — the form or landing page where the contact was captured
    - **Custom Field** — any field value stored on the contact record

    The audience panel shows a live count of matching contacts as you apply filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build Your Sequence">
    Click **Add Step** to insert the first message in your sequence. For each step:

    1. Write your **Subject Line** (email) or **Message Text** (SMS).
    2. Compose the **Email Body** using the rich-text editor or paste raw HTML.
    3. Set the **Send Delay** — choose **Immediately** for the first step, then **+1 day**, **+3 days**, or any custom interval for subsequent steps.

    Repeat until your full sequence is built. Drag steps to reorder them at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview on Desktop and Mobile">
    Click **Preview** on any step to see exactly how your message will render. Toggle between **Desktop** and **Mobile** views to catch formatting issues before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate Your Campaign">
    When you are satisfied with the sequence, click the status toggle in the top-right corner and set the campaign to **Active**. Google will begin enrolling matching contacts immediately and send messages according to your defined delays.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Writing Effective Messages

Personalization dramatically improves open and click-through rates. Insert dynamic tokens anywhere in your subject line or body to automatically replace placeholder text with real contact data.

| Token | Resolves to |
|---|---|
| `{{first_name}}` | Contact's first name |
| `{{company}}` | Company name on the contact record |
| `{{site_name}}` | Your Google account display name |

Here is an example email body that uses personalization tokens:

```text
Hi {{first_name}},

I noticed you checked out our pricing page earlier this week — 
thanks for your interest!

I'd love to show you how {{site_name}} has helped companies like 
{{company}} increase conversions by 30% in the first 60 days.

Would you be open to a 20-minute call this week?

Best,
The {{site_name}} Team
```

If a token has no value for a given contact (e.g., the company field is blank), Google substitutes a fallback you define under **Settings → Personalization Defaults**.

## A/B Testing

A/B testing lets you run a controlled experiment on any step in your campaign sequence to find the message that converts best.

**To enable an A/B test:**

1. Hover over any campaign step and click **A/B Test**.
2. Define **Variant B** by writing an alternative subject line, changing the email body, or adjusting the send time — you can test one variable at a time.
3. Set the **Traffic Split** — for example, 50% of recipients see Variant A and 50% see Variant B.
4. Let the test run until each variant has been delivered to at least **200 recipients** to reach statistical significance.

Once the test completes, Google highlights the winning variant in your campaign analytics. Click **Apply Winner** to replace the original step with the better-performing version.

<Note>
  Google automatically appends a legally compliant unsubscribe link to every outbound email, satisfying CAN-SPAM and GDPR requirements. You do not need to add one manually. SMS messages include an opt-out instruction ("Reply STOP to unsubscribe") by default.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Emails sent on **Tuesday or Wednesday between 9 AM and 11 AM** in the recipient's local time zone consistently see the highest open rates across industries. Use Google's **Send Time Optimization** toggle (available on any step) to let the platform automatically schedule delivery in each contact's best window.
</Tip>
