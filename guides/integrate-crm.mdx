Your CRM is where deals get closed — but it is only as useful as the data flowing into it. Connecting Google to your CRM means every lead captured through your forms, pop-ups, and landing pages appears instantly in the tool your sales team already lives in. This guide walks you through connecting a native CRM integration, using the API or Zapier for unsupported platforms, and understanding exactly how sync behaves once everything is live.

## Connecting HubSpot

The steps below use HubSpot as the primary example. The connection flow for Salesforce and Pipedrive is nearly identical — substitute the relevant platform name at each step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the HubSpot Integration Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations → HubSpot** in your Google dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the Connection">
    Click **Connect HubSpot**. You will be redirected to HubSpot's OAuth authorization screen. Sign in to your HubSpot account and click **Grant Access** to allow Google to read and write contact data. You will be returned to your Google dashboard automatically once authorization is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map Your Fields">
    In the **Field Mapping** panel, match Google lead properties to the corresponding HubSpot Contact properties. At minimum, configure the following mappings:

    | Google Field | HubSpot Property |
    |---|---|
    | Name | `firstname` / `lastname` |
    | Email | `email` |
    | Phone | `phone` |
    | Tags | `google_tags` (custom property) |

    Click **Add Mapping** to include any additional custom fields your team tracks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Sync Direction">
    Choose how data flows between the two platforms:

    - **One-Way (Google → HubSpot)** — lead data flows from Google into HubSpot only. Changes made in HubSpot are not reflected in Google.
    - **Two-Way** — updates in either platform are mirrored to the other. This is recommended if your sales team edits contact records directly in HubSpot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Go Live">
    Click **Save Integration**. Google will immediately begin syncing new leads to HubSpot as they are captured. Existing leads are not synced retroactively by default — use the **Bulk Sync** button to push your historical contacts on demand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connecting via API or Zapier

If your CRM is not listed above, you have two options:

**REST API** — Google exposes a full REST API for leads. You can POST lead data to your CRM directly, query and update contact records, and subscribe to webhook events. See the [Leads API reference](/api/leads) for endpoint documentation, authentication details, and request examples.

**Zapier** — For a no-code solution, use the Google Zapier app:

1. Log in to Zapier and click **Create Zap**.
2. Set the **Trigger** app to **Google** and choose the **New Lead Captured** event.
3. Connect your Google account and select the form or lead source you want to monitor.
4. Set the **Action** app to your CRM (e.g., Zoho, ActiveCampaign, Copper) and choose **Create Contact** or the equivalent action.
5. Map the fields and turn the Zap on.

Every new lead captured in Google will instantly create a contact record in your chosen CRM — no code required.

## Sync Behavior

Understanding how and when data syncs helps you avoid duplicate records and data gaps:

- **New leads** sync to your CRM in **real time**, typically within a few seconds of capture.
- **Updates to existing records** (e.g., a tag added via a workflow, or a status change) sync on a **5-minute interval**.
- **Deleted leads** in Google are **not deleted** in your CRM. Instead, Google adds a `google-deleted` tag to the contact record in your CRM so your team is aware of the change without losing the record.
- If a sync attempt fails (e.g., due to a field validation error in the CRM), Google logs the error under **Settings → Integrations → Sync Log** and retries automatically after 15 minutes.

<Tip>
  Map all of your custom fields **before** going live with the integration. Fields that are not mapped at the time a lead syncs will not be back-filled automatically — you would need to trigger a manual re-sync for any affected records. Take ten minutes to audit your field mapping and confirm each custom field is correctly paired before saving and activating the integration.
</Tip>
