This guide walks you through everything you need to capture your first lead and trigger an automated follow-up. By the end, you'll have a live lead form on your site, a verified conversion event in your dashboard, and a welcome email campaign ready to engage new prospects.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have the following:

- A **Google account** — sign up free at [app.google.com](https://app.google.com) if you don't have one yet.
- A **website or landing page** where you can add a JavaScript snippet to the `<head>` of your HTML.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to [app.google.com](https://app.google.com) and click **Get Started Free**. Enter your name, work email address, and a password, then click **Create Account**.

    Google will send a verification email to the address you provided. Open that email and click **Verify My Email** to activate your account and land in your new workspace dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the Tracking Snippet">
    The Google tracking snippet identifies visitors on your site and powers all lead capture, analytics, and conversion tracking.

    From your dashboard, go to **Settings → Tracking Snippet** and copy your unique snippet. Paste it inside the `<head>` tag of every page on your site — or, if you use a tag manager, add it as a new tag.

    ```html
    <script
      src="https://cdn.google.com/conversion.js"
      data-site-id="YOUR_SITE_ID"
    ></script>
    ```

    Replace `YOUR_SITE_ID` with the value shown in your dashboard. This value is unique to your workspace.

    <Note>
      The tracking snippet only needs to be installed once. A single snippet placed in the shared `<head>` of your site covers all pages automatically — no need to add it page by page.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your First Lead Form">
    With tracking live, you're ready to start capturing leads. In your dashboard, navigate to **Capture → Forms → New Form**.

    1. Give your form a name (for example, "Homepage Exit Intent Form").
    2. Select the fields you want to collect — **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** are available by default.
    3. Under **Trigger**, choose when the form appears:
       - **Exit Intent** — displays when the visitor moves their cursor toward the browser tab bar.
       - **Time on Page** — displays after a set number of seconds (for example, 30 seconds).
    4. Customize the headline and submit button text to match your offer.
    5. Click **Publish** to make the form live on your site.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify It's Working">
    Open your website in a new browser tab (use an incognito window to simulate a fresh visitor). Trigger the form using whichever method you configured — move your cursor to the top of the screen for exit intent, or wait for the time delay.

    Fill in the form fields with test data and click submit.

    Back in the Google dashboard, go to **Dashboard → Leads**. Your test submission should appear within a few seconds. If you see it listed, your tracking snippet and form are working correctly.

    <Tip>
      Use a real email address you have access to for your test lead. This lets you also verify that any automated follow-up emails are delivered and formatted correctly.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up a Follow-Up Campaign">
    Now that you're capturing leads, put them into an automated welcome sequence. Navigate to **Campaigns → New Campaign**.

    1. **Name your campaign** (for example, "New Lead Welcome Series").
    2. Under **Audience**, select the lead form you just created as the source — all new submissions will automatically enter this campaign.
    3. Click **Add Email**, write your welcome message, and set a send delay (for example, send immediately or after 5 minutes).
    4. Preview the email, then click **Activate Campaign**.

    From this point on, every new lead captured by your form will automatically receive your welcome email.
  </Step>
</Steps>
