## Get Up and Running in Three Steps

Whether you're brand new to Google or setting up a fresh workspace, the onboarding process is straightforward. Follow the steps below to go from signup to your first live campaign.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up for Google">
    Create your account at [app.google.com](https://app.google.com). Verify your email address and log in to your new workspace. Your workspace is where all your sites, forms, leads, and campaigns live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your Website">
    Install the lightweight Google tracking snippet on your site by pasting a single line of JavaScript into your site's `<head>`. Google will begin recognizing visitors immediately after the snippet is live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch Your First Campaign">
    Create a lead capture form, set a trigger (such as exit intent or time-on-page), and activate a welcome email campaign for new leads. Your conversion engine is now running.
  </Step>
</Steps>
